GEO market-entry framework

Pioneered the agency's GEO market entry strategy, turning a pilot test into a client-ready commercial offering.

via Propeller, Account Director

Pioneered the agency's GEO market entry strategy, turning a pilot test into a client-ready commercial offering with a GTM and attribution framework.

Owned the agency's overall PR and growth strategy — positioning, target markets, content and measurement — including a structured US market-entry play targeting AdTech companies and US-scaling independent agencies, built on detailed ICPs, tiered target-account lists, buyer personas and a competitive positioning audit across UK and US markets.

Led the strategic push into GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation), spotting early that buyers increasingly discover agencies through AI tools, and built the measurement capability to prove it: a three-layer framework spanning AI search (Peec AI), traditional search (Google Search Console) and on-site behaviour (GA4), with automated monthly benchmarking, live dashboards and a client-facing report.
