Castore — Murray Mile

A guerrilla activation marking Andy Murray's final Wimbledon, turned into a nationally-covered news story.

via PrettyGreen, Group Marketing Manager

Delivered the on-the-ground activation for Castore's "Murray Mile" campaign, marking Andy Murray's final Wimbledon by petitioning to rename the Church Road approach to SW19. Scouted the Wimbledon location, supported the campaign shoot, and shaped the guerrilla marketing strategy that turned a low-budget stunt into a nationally-covered sporting news story.

Wrote and placed the campaign case study with trade press, securing coverage in LBB Online and The Drum.
